# Recruitment Office Briefing

## Minimal pattern

```text
Use the Recruitment Office.
Load my Career Context and current Job Search Context.
I want: [new role / career change / applications / interviews / salary negotiation].
Targets: [roles/sectors/locations if known].
Constraints: [minimum compensation, remote/relocation, timing, etc.].
```

## Example — find jobs

```text
Use the Recruitment Office.
Find current roles that would be a material career improvement for me.
Use my Career Context, but do not infer protected personal traits.
Verify active roles at original sources, deduplicate them, and rank my attention by documented fit, career upside and application effort. Do not submit anything yet.
```

## Example — application

```text
Use the Recruitment Office.
Assess this role against my Career Context.
If worth pursuing, tailor my CV and portfolio emphasis using only verified facts, draft the application, fact-check it, and add the role to the application tracker. Wait for my approval before submission.
```

## Example — offer

```text
Use the Recruitment Office and Legal Office.
Compare this offer with my current career goals and compensation target. Recruitment Office handles career/compensation strategy; Legal Office reviews the employment terms and restrictive covenants.
```
