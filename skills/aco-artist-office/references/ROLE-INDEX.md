# artist-office — role index

Lead: `creative_orchestrator`. Read only selected role instructions; native names use `aco_`.

- [`art_contracts_rights_advisor`](agents/art-contracts-rights-advisor.md) — First-pass contracts, rights and deal-terms reviewer for artists: gallery agreements, commissions, licenses, image rights, editions, acquisitions, exclusivity, AI/data clauses and production responsibilities.
- [`art_critic`](agents/art-critic.md) — Independent contemporary-art critic for rigorous evaluation, originality checks, conceptual pressure-testing, close reading and counter-interpretation.
- [`art_ecosystem_analyst`](agents/art-ecosystem-analyst.md) — Analyst of contemporary-art institutions, galleries, fairs, biennials, residencies, prizes, funding, representation, networks and professional positioning.
- [`art_historian`](agents/art-historian.md) — Art historian and theory researcher for historical genealogies, movements, precedents, concepts, bibliographies and source-critical contextualization.
- [`art_marketing_director`](agents/art-marketing-director.md) — Marketing director specialized in contemporary artists, exhibitions, cultural projects and art platforms; develops audience, campaign, launch and channel strategies without flattening art into generic content marketing.
- [`art_opportunities_scout`](agents/art-opportunities-scout.md) — Live opportunity scout for contemporary artists: residencies, grants, prizes, commissions, public art, exhibitions, research programmes, teaching and institutional open calls, verified from official sources.
- [`art_publicist`](agents/art-publicist.md) — Contemporary-art publicist for press strategy, media mapping, story angles, press materials, journalist outreach, interviews, launches and cultural partnerships.
- [`art_researcher`](agents/art-researcher.md) — Current contemporary-art researcher for artists, exhibitions, institutions, discourse, themes, references, and cultural context. Use when claims need evidence and up-to-date sources.
- [`art_sales_director`](agents/art-sales-director.md) — Primary-market art sales strategist for pricing, editions, offers, collector development, gallery coordination, sales pipelines, negotiations and sustainable artwork revenue.
- [`artist_agent`](agents/artist-agent.md) — Artist representative and career-development agent for contemporary artists: positioning, opportunities, institutional relationships, gallery strategy, outreach, negotiation preparation and long-term career planning.
- [`artist_revenue_strategist`](agents/artist-revenue-strategist.md) — Revenue strategy lead for a contemporary artist: diversifies sustainable income across sales, editions, commissions, fees, grants, licensing, publications, teaching and other practice-compatible streams without distorting the practice.
- [`artist_social_media_manager`](agents/artist-social-media-manager.md) — Social media manager specialized in contemporary artists and art profiles; handles profile strategy, editorial calendars, captions, launches, documentation and audience growth while preserving artistic credibility.
- [`artwork_documentation_manager`](agents/artwork-documentation-manager.md) — Artwork and exhibition documentation manager for photography/video standards, shot lists, filenames, metadata, captions, credits, condition imagery, masters and delivery packages.
- [`collection_inventory_manager`](agents/collection-inventory-manager.md) — Operational artwork inventory manager: availability, location, ownership, consignment, reservation, price, condition status, exhibition status and movement-ready records.
- [`collector_relations_manager`](agents/collector-relations-manager.md) — Collector and patron relationship manager for contemporary artists: CRM structure, private communications, follow-up, stewardship, viewings, acquisition journeys and long-term relationship development.
- [`commissions_licensing_manager`](agents/commissions-licensing-manager.md) — Manager for artist commissions, licensing and reproduction rights: structures scope, fees, deliverables, usage, approvals, exclusivity, renewals and rights protections.
- [`creative_orchestrator`](agents/creative-orchestrator.md) — Orchestrator for a full contemporary-art studio and professional office: artistic research, curatorial development, career, practice planning, opportunities, production, rights, revenue, sales, marketing, communications and operations.
- [`curator`](agents/curator.md) — Contemporary-art curator for exhibition concepts, artist selection, commissions, spatial logic, interpretive frameworks, public programme and curatorial writing.
- [`digital_preservation_manager`](agents/digital-preservation-manager.md) — Digital/time-based artwork preservation specialist for masters, checksums, codecs, software dependencies, models, environments, migration, backups and future re-installability.
- [`edition_manager`](agents/edition-manager.md) — Edition-control specialist for photographs, prints, video, software and other reproducible works: edition structures, APs, certificates, numbering, production specs, availability and price ladders.
- [`exhibition_producer`](agents/exhibition-producer.md) — Exhibition and installation producer for contemporary art: converts artistic concepts into technical riders, fabrication, AV, logistics, budgets, schedules, installation/deinstallation and venue coordination.
- [`fabrication_manager`](agents/fabrication-manager.md) — Physical-artwork fabrication manager for vendor sourcing, RFQs, materials, prototypes, tolerances, drawings, quality control, schedules, costs and handoff to installation.
- [`gallery_relations_manager`](agents/gallery-relations-manager.md) — Gallery-facing relationship and representation manager: representation talks, consignments, exclusivity, territories, fairs, inventory, reporting, commissions and gallery follow-up.
- [`grants_applications_manager`](agents/grants-applications-manager.md) — Grant and application manager for artists: builds compliant, persuasive applications, budgets, timelines, statements, supporting-material checklists and submission plans without distorting the practice to fit a call.
- [`institutional_relations_manager`](agents/institutional-relations-manager.md) — Relationship manager for curators, museums, foundations, biennials, universities, publishers and cultural institutions; maintains context, follow-ups, introductions and long-term institutional relationships.
- [`practice_archivist`](agents/practice-archivist.md) — Canonical archive and portfolio manager for an artist practice: works, exhibitions, images, editions, credits, dimensions, technical data, texts, press, CV, bios and portfolio variants.
- [`practice_strategist`](agents/practice-strategist.md) — Senior strategy agent for an artist practice: periodically reviews artistic development, exhibitions, research, relationships, applications, sales, visibility, workload and opportunity cost to keep the practice coherent and moving forward.
- [`press_reputation_researcher`](agents/press-reputation-researcher.md) — Due-diligence and context researcher for art-world people and institutions: recent activity, programmes, artists, writing, press, controversies, mutual context and evidence-based contact briefs.
- [`publication_manager`](agents/publication-manager.md) — Artist-publication production manager for monographs, catalogues, artist books and editorial projects: contributors, captions, permissions, proofing, print, ISBN, budgets, schedules and distribution.
- [`registrar_art_logistics`](agents/registrar-art-logistics.md) — Registrar and art-logistics specialist for artwork movement, packing, shipping, customs, insurance, loans, condition reports, storage, couriers and installation logistics.
- [`studio_chief_of_staff`](agents/studio-chief-of-staff.md) — Chief of staff for an artist studio: translates approved practice strategy into 90-day, annual and weekly execution while coordinating projects, opportunities, production, relationships, revenue and studio operations.
- [`studio_comms_manager`](agents/studio-comms-manager.md) — Artist-studio communications operations manager for routine inbound/outbound requests, triage, image/bio/material delivery, scheduling, admin follow-up and response tracking.
- [`studio_research_librarian`](agents/studio-research-librarian.md) — Research-library and knowledge-organization specialist for books, papers, catalogues, PDFs, notes, references, citations, images and durable studio research retrieval.
- [`technical_art_producer`](agents/technical-art-producer.md) — Technical producer for AI, video, interactive, web and computational artworks; turns artistic intent into robust system architectures, prototypes, hardware/software plans and exhibition-ready technical solutions.
