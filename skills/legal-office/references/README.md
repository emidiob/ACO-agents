# Legal Office

A generic, jurisdiction-aware legal-support office for contract review/drafting, IP and licensing, privacy/data, AI/technology law, employment/contractor issues, compliance, dispute prevention and legal operations.

It is designed to support—not impersonate—licensed counsel. For current law and high-impact matters it requires authoritative-source verification and clear escalation.

## One-line invocation

> Use the Legal Office.

## Typical context

```text
company-context.md / artist-context.md / client-context.md (as relevant)
+ legal-context.md
+ legal-matter-context.md
+ the actual contract/document
+ current brief
```

## Cross-office examples
- Artist agreement: Artist Office + Legal Office.
- Client/SOW/MSA: Agency Office + Legal Office.
- Privacy/AI feature: Product Office + Legal Office.
- Employment offer/contract: Recruitment Office + Legal Office.
