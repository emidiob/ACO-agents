# Legal Office Briefing

## Minimal pattern

```text
Use the Legal Office.
Jurisdiction / governing law: [if known]
Task: [review / draft / research / negotiate / organize]
Objective: [what outcome you want]
Documents: [paths/files]
Deadline: [if any]
Risk/importance: [context]
```

If jurisdiction, actual document text or a key factual point is decisive, the office should ask for it rather than guess.

## Example — contract review

```text
Use the Legal Office.
Review this services agreement from the supplier side.
Identify governing law, payment, scope, IP, usage rights, liability, indemnity, termination and dispute terms.
Give me: must-fix issues, negotiable issues, proposed fallback language, and questions that require local counsel.
```

## Example — artist rights

```text
Use the Artist Office and Legal Office.
Load Artist Context only for deal/practice facts.
Review the acquisition/license proposal and protect artistic/usage rights without changing the commercial goal.
```
