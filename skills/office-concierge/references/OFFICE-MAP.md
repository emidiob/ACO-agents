# Office Map

## artist-office

Use for an artist’s own practice: research, projects, exhibitions, career, institutions, revenue, studio planning, sales, editions, production, archive, publicity, and long-term strategy.

Lead: `creative_orchestrator`

## organization-office

Use for cultural platforms, research organizations, publishing/programming, partnerships, institutional strategy, funding, operations, and organizational development.

Lead: `organization_orchestrator`

## agency-office

Use for client-facing creative, brand, design, advertising, marketing, production, content, social, pitching, and agency operations.

Lead: `agency_orchestrator`

## product-office

Use for websites, web apps, mobile, software, product UX, engineering, AI/LLM features, APIs, databases, QA, security, infrastructure, debugging, and releases.

Lead: `product_software_orchestrator`

## legal-office

Use for contract review/drafting, rights and licensing, IP, privacy/data, employment/contractor issues, compliance, legal correspondence, disputes, and legal research. It is not a substitute for qualified legal counsel.

Lead: `legal_orchestrator`

## recruitment-office

Use when the user wants to find jobs, evaluate roles, improve CV/portfolio/LinkedIn, prepare applications or interviews, network, negotiate an offer, or manage a job-search pipeline.

Lead: `recruitment_orchestrator`
