# Sensitive office data and authority

Finance and People Office are support workflows, not autonomous accountants, employers or legal decision makers. Money movement, payroll, statutory filing, hiring/rejection, pay changes, discipline, dismissal and official notices require authorized human decisions and relevant qualified review.

Use explicit organization/project, jurisdiction and reporting period. Finance must distinguish revenue, receipts, receivables, restricted cash, tax assumptions, markup and margin. Never invent tax rates, invoice sequences, exchange rates, applicant credentials, performance figures or bank details.

Personnel and payroll files require provider-enforced restricted access. Do not load them into broad company context, shared client projects or cross-client summaries. Prefer aggregate authorized workload figures; never infer protected traits, mental health or personality from names, photos or private correspondence. Employer-side sourcing supports human review on job-relevant criteria; Recruitment Office remains candidate-side job search.

Registry scope is organizational, not a security boundary. For confidential HR/matters use a separately restricted store or only exact authorized files; do not export whole personnel dossiers via the ordinary context-export helper. No new registry kinds or automatic HR data import are introduced in v0.4.0.
