# Role and skill stocktake

Use this during ACO library maintenance, not during ordinary user work. The purpose is to improve quality without turning every gap into a new persona.

## Review order
1. Inventory changed roles / skills first; periodically review the full library.
2. Check overlap with other roles, shared protocols and playbooks.
3. Verify current technical / legal / platform references when they are time-sensitive.
4. Check whether the role's trigger, method, deliverable and verification are concrete.
5. Review routing: an excellent role that is never selected may need better Concierge mapping, not another role.

## Verdicts
- **Keep** — distinct, current and actionable.
- **Improve** — useful but method / trigger / output / verification needs work.
- **Update** — external facts or tooling are stale.
- **Merge** — substantial overlap; preserve the unique method in the stronger target.
- **Retire** — no meaningful unique value remains.

Every Merge / Retire decision must name the replacement and impact. Never delete roles or files automatically from an audit; present the exact proposed change for review.

## Evidence
Use concrete evidence: duplicate text, broken links, stale source versions, failed behavior evals, repeated routing errors, or lack of unique method. Low usage alone is not enough to retire a rare but critical specialist.
