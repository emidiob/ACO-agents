# Residency, public commission and opportunity fit

Original ACO operational method · v0.6.0. Select for the relevant task; do not load every playbook.

## Start from a need
Ask what access is missing: making time, critical exchange, archive, facilities, research partners, production resources or a relevant public. An external deadline is not a reason to invent a new practice. A residency, grant and public commission have different obligations.

## Verify the actual call
A directory is a discovery source. Follow the original provider's current call and retain a dated reference. Verify discipline, residence/nationality constraints where relevant, career criteria, application route, deadline with timezone, duration, travel, accessibility, fees, eligible costs, artist fee, production budget, payment timing, rights and expected outcomes. Unknown eligibility is unknown, not a pass. Do not infer a programme's current terms from a past edition.

## Evaluate fit without prestige scoring
Describe the practice need served, artistic freedom, useful relationships and the opportunity cost. Separate total award from disposable artist income and committed production costs. Compare attendance requirements with the user's actual capacity; do not assume a salaried leave arrangement. A public-art call also requires site access, maintenance responsibilities, community process, fabrication, insurability and public-procurement/legal review when relevant.

## Make a decision
Return pursue / investigate / decline with reasons and uncertainties. If a hard eligibility requirement is false or the deadline passed, do not disguise it as a strong lead. Never invent selection probability. A paid or unremunerated opportunity can be evaluated, but its cost and the artist's preferences must be explicit. Rejecting it is a legitimate outcome.

## Application production
Reuse verified work samples, CV and project information. Build a requirements-to-evidence checklist, costed schedule and final submission checklist only for a chosen call. Drafting a proposal does not confirm the project. Submit only through an available authorized route and retain the actual receipt. Follow-up status should reflect provider evidence, not optimism.

## Storage
Discovery stays in chat by default. Retained opportunities occupy compact pipeline rows in the existing owner document. Preparing or submitting a PDF permits that deliverable, not a new set of memory folders. Acceptance must be verified and the artist's commitment clarified before promotion. Rejection changes one status and keeps only useful feedback.
