# CRO, SEO and measurement without invented causality

## Intake
Business objective, audience, conversion definition, channel, existing analytics and permission to inspect data. A proposal can be drafted without baseline data, but its effect must not be stated as measured.

## Method
1. Trace one user journey from acquisition to an actual value event. Separate discovery, comprehension, trust, interaction friction and delivery problems.
2. Ground the hypothesis in available evidence: user observations, funnel data, queries or a reproducible interface problem. Label unsupported opportunities as hypotheses.
3. For discoverability, inspect crawl access, status codes, canonical URLs, indexability, internal linking, page intent, metadata and structured data against source content. Do not promise rankings or fabricate schema claims/reviews.
4. Define analytics events before drawing conclusions: trigger, properties, exclusions, consent, identity scope, deduplication and ownership. Keep sensitive payloads out of event properties. Test firing and absence under opt-out.
5. For an experiment, predefine eligibility, unit of assignment, primary metric, guardrails, minimum duration/sample rationale and stop/rollback criteria. Do not invent sample size when baseline/effect assumptions are missing.
6. Change a coherent causal factor. Separate observed lift from seasonality, traffic mix and instrumentation changes. Avoid repeated peeking or post-hoc outcome selection.
7. For lifecycle communications, record audience consent, exclusions, suppression, channel and approval. Drafting sequences is not authorization to send them.

## Outputs
Evidence-ranked backlog, instrumented event specification, experiment plan and analysis with uncertainty. Preserve brand and editorial independence; no deceptive urgency, fake testimonials or unauthorized bulk messaging.
