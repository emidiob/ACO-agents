# Artistic research and contemporary-art source desk

Original ACO operational method · v0.6.0. Select for the relevant task; do not load every playbook.

## Choose the research mode
Clarify whether the task is contextualization, a genealogy, a visual comparison, a field enquiry, technical experimentation or a speculative proposition. Do not force practice-led inquiry into a literature review. Separate primary material, interpretation, theory, institutional publicity and market news.

## Search and read
Start with a question and a bounded set of source types; do not search a whole personal library without need. Read the material behind a citation. Maintain title/author/date, stable URL or collection identifier, locator and a short note explaining relevance and limits. Keep an assertion-to-source trail for factual claims. An AI-generated bibliography or summary is not a verified source.

For contemporary discourse, consult relevant criticism and artist writing rather than only open calls. Use e-flux Journal/Criticism, ArtReview, Frieze and Afterall as starting points, not an exhaustive canon. Add local-language criticism, artist-run publications and primary programmes in the regions under discussion. Distinguish an institutional announcement or sponsored feature from an independent review. Repeated promotion of one artist is not proof of a field-wide trend.

## Compare responsibly
Describe what is actually visible in artworks and images. Check provenance of reproductions, captions and rights. Museum open data does not automatically license every linked image; rights need item-level checking. Comparative similarity does not prove influence or plagiarism. Negative and contradictory examples belong in the synthesis.

## Tool choice
Use existing bibliography and archive tools first. Zotero can be an external bibliography; Tropy can organize research photographs; IIIF/Mirador can support image comparison; Omeka is an optional publication layer. These are separate jobs, not a required stack. No automatic migration, bulk crawl, model training, cloud upload or institution-scale collection system for a small studio enquiry. If no tool is connected, a small cited reading list in chat is enough.

## Return something the practice can use
Give a short map of positions, a material/visual observation, the strongest disagreement and an experiment or question for the studio. State what was not accessed. Do not present research as a finished artwork or guarantee originality.

## Memory and copyright
Keep bibliography in its existing home and link selected records from ACO.md. Do not save a Drive file for every paper, article or tool. Private notes and copyrighted full text do not belong in the public ACO repository. Never bypass paywalls or publish source excerpts beyond permitted use. The source desk stores references, not downloaded journal archives.
