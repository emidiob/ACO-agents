# Evidence-led critique and peer dialogue

Original ACO operational method · v0.6.0. Select for the relevant task; do not load every playbook.

## Intake
Confirm what can actually be seen or heard: files, dimensions, duration, installation conditions and whether the material is a finished work, documentation, proposal or simulation. Name any missing aspect. Ask whether the artist wants a close reading, a challenge to the premise, or practical revision.

## Independence
For a blind pass, pass only the selected work and neutral technical information into an execution that has not received identity, career history or expected themes. If this conversation already contains that context, say the pass is context-limited, not genuinely blind. Do not simulate a panel of named real curators or imply human experts reviewed the work.

## Method
Describe observable choices before interpretation. Separate what the material does from what the statement claims. Develop two plausible readings and one counter-reading; tie each to a visible, temporal, spatial or sonic feature. Test the relationship between form and claim, the role of the viewer, what is derivative or unresolved, and what would be lost by simplifying it. Do not penalize deliberate opacity merely for being difficult.

Ask questions an artist can use: which decision is doing the most work; where attention changes; whether a reference deepens or replaces an encounter; what remains after the explanation is removed. When giving an opinion, label it as an interpretation, and obtain permission before unusually prescriptive recommendations.

Only then load the artist's intentions, if requested, and compare. Preserve disagreement rather than retrofitting the initial reading to the approved narrative. Suggest one or two experiments, not a replacement artwork written by the agent.

## Human session facilitation
Agree on participants, confidentiality, duration and how feedback will be used. Invite observations, artist questions, neutral questions and permission-based suggestions. Record actual comments and speakers only when provided or consented to. Never invent dialogue, consensus, quotations or attendance.

## Output and retention
Return observations, interpretations, a productive disagreement and next experiments. Keep only artist-selected durable feedback in the current ACO.md. Do not generate a document for each comment or rejected direction. A critique report is a deliverable only if asked for.
