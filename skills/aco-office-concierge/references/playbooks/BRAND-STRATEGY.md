# Brand strategy with evidence and room to change

Original ACO operational method · v0.6.0. Select for the relevant task; do not load every playbook.

## Start with the decision
Identify whether this is an existing brand audit, a new offer, a repositioning or a fictional exploration. Load the approved organization/brand scope and current material. Do not require a long discovery survey for a narrow task. Ask only the information that changes the recommendation.

## Evidence map
Separate internal beliefs, observed customer/audience evidence, independent category research and assumptions. Examine what people currently choose instead, including doing nothing. Do not fabricate interviews, personas, market shares, testimonials or research findings. Competitor repetition of the same claim is a category convention, not a proven audience need.

## Strategic options
Define audience/problem, offer, distinct value, credible proof and consequences of choosing each option. Explain what the brand will not claim or pursue. Test the proposition against the real offer and delivery capacity. Purpose, values and personality are optional working tools, not mandatory abstract paragraphs. Cultural organizations may prioritize editorial independence and public value instead of conversion.

Create two or three materially different positions only when the decision calls for alternatives. Separate creative language from substantiated claims. An attractive tagline is not evidence that a position is true. A territory can be proposed, researched or validated; record the stage rather than assigning fake confidence percentages.

## Downstream contract
Once a human selects a route, give messaging hierarchy, evidence for each claim, voice principles and the implications for identity, service and channels. Mark unapproved positioning explicitly so downstream copy does not harden it into truth. Derive measures from the actual objective: recognition, comprehension, enquiry quality or another user-approved outcome. Do not treat reach or follower count as universal brand equity.

## Deliverable and memory
Return a decision-ready brief, a claim/evidence table and the next validation step. Store the approved compact Brand section inside the existing owner ACO.md. A client-facing strategy deck is a legitimate deliverable when requested; the memory layer stores a link, not a second set of strategy files. Revisions supersede dated decisions rather than deleting their history.
