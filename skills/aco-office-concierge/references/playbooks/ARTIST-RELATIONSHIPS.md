# Artist representation, studio visits and institutional relationships

Original ACO operational method · v0.6.0. Select for the relevant task; do not load every playbook.

## Scope
Support an artist's professional relationships without pretending to represent them legally or know private intentions of galleries, curators or collectors. Separate institutional collaboration, critical dialogue and a sales relationship.

## Build a contact brief, not a bulk mailing list
Use public professional material or the user's authorized correspondence. Verify the current role and relevant programme before using a person's name. Separate known contact history, observed affinity and your hypothesis. A public profile or exhibition theme is not evidence of a personal relationship, buying capacity or interest in this artist.

For a proposed contact, answer: why this person; which actual work/programme makes the connection plausible; what the artist could share now; what makes the timing meaningful; and whether any prior refusal or conflict prevents outreach. Prefer a few well-grounded relationships to indiscriminate lead volume.

## Sequence
Prepare a short, specific invitation, introduction request or update. State the purpose of a studio visit and the material to show, with a small portfolio rather than a large attachment dump. Do not add people to newsletters without the appropriate consent. Existing known relationships can be handled warmly without inventing memories.

After a visit or reply, distinguish the person's actual feedback from interpretation. Record any agreed next action and timing. No reply is not rejection and is not permission for aggressive follow-ups. Respect an explicit refusal and do not switch from email to WhatsApp to bypass it.

## Representation and sales handoff
Before advising on a gallery relationship, clarify territory, exclusivity, commission, costs, consignment, reporting, insurance and termination questions from the actual draft. Do not equate representation with guaranteed sales or sustained income. Route binding terms to the appropriate legal adviser. Collector conversations should preserve price consistency, edition truth and the artist's decision on availability.

## Output
A concise contact brief and one copy-ready message, or a recommendation not to approach. An actual send needs verified account, recipient and approval. The source of contact information stays private. Update one existing relationship/pipeline row; a researched person does not become a client folder or a permanently scored human profile.
