# Whiteboard and diagrams

Use rough diagrams to think, align and hand off—not as proof of implementation.

- Choose one purpose: architecture, user flow, service map, wireframe, storyboard or workshop.
- Label assumptions, boundaries and unresolved connections directly on the diagram.
- Keep shapes and notation simple enough for the intended audience.
- Link/export only materially useful diagrams; ephemeral sketches can stay ephemeral.
- For technical diagrams, verify the final architecture against source/config before treating it as canonical.
