# Human writing review

Optional final pass for public-facing prose. Preserve the author rather than manufacturing a generic “human” voice.

- Lead with the actual claim when that improves the genre.
- Replace vague importance claims, unsupported consensus and filler with concrete evidence or delete them.
- Remove repetitive rhetorical templates, fake profundity and unnecessary scene-setting when they are not intentional.
- Prefer specific nouns/verbs and sentence rhythm appropriate to the author and medium.
- Preserve deliberate ambiguity, repetition, fragments, dialect and stylistic devices when they serve the work.
- Never infer whether a text was written by AI from style alone.
- Fact-check factual claims separately; style cleanup is not verification.
