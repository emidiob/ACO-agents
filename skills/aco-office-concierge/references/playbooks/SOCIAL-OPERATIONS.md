# Social planning, production, publishing and learning

Original ACO operational method · v0.6.0. Select for the relevant task; do not load every playbook.

## First establish purpose and scope
Identify owner/brand, account, audience, objective and available material. For an artist, protect artistic intent, appropriate context and publication consent; a private study is not automatically content. Do not turn every practice into a personal-brand growth funnel. Confirm which platforms genuinely deserve effort and whether the request is strategy, drafting, scheduling or actual posting.

## Plan from evidence and capacity
Use the compact approved Brand/Social sections and any real analytics. Select a few content pillars tied to the objective, then a sustainable cadence and a small batch. Distinguish reusable source material, platform-specific adaptation and newly commissioned production. Do not fabricate current trends, algorithmic certainty, statistics or testimonial quotes. No universal best posting time: label an initial hypothesis and test with the account's evidence.

## Production packet
For each post retain an ID, account/brand, platform, format, objective, final text, actual media references, alt text/captions when relevant, rights/disclosure status, claims and their sources, approval state and intended timing. A carousel needs a coherent sequence; a short video needs an action/argument and an edit, not merely a catchy first line. Adapt rather than paste identical copy everywhere. Do not invent engagement by bots, false controversy or fake personal experience.

## Publish gate
Use the actual connected provider first. Inspect supported operations for this account and platform: a scheduling UI, SDK or MCP name does not establish that the current token can post. Check current limits from first-party documentation or actual provider validation. Bind the human approval to the exact account, content version, media and schedule. Modified copy/media/timing invalidates the previous publication approval. Verify rights, required disclosures and sensitive/private information before sending.

After executing, distinguish queued, scheduled, published, failed and unknown using real provider results. Scheduling acceptance is not proof of publication. Reconcile an uncertain response before retrying. Do not cross-post or switch accounts without authorization. If unavailable, return copy-ready text and media/timing instructions with NOT PUBLISHED outside the copy.

## Community and review
Answer from verified policy; route complaints, threats, safeguarding and legal matters to humans. Respect opt-outs. Review outcomes against a defined baseline and time window, noting paid promotion and confounders. Report denominators; do not invent attributable revenue. Choose a next experiment rather than declaring virality.

## Memory
Keep the calendar in its existing authorized tool or one compact table. Individual draft posts, trend ideas and campaign proposals do not each create Drive files. Store only approved strategy, material outcomes and canonical asset/provider links in ACO.md. No recurring schedule exists until a real scheduler confirms it.
