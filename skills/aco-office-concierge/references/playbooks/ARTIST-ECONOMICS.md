# Artist income without surrendering the practice

Original ACO operational method · v0.6.0. Select for the relevant task; do not load every playbook.

## Start from constraints, not merchandise
Identify the artist's financial objective, available time, existing work and protected artistic boundaries. Separate living costs, production funds, gross sales, artist fees, pass-through expenses and disposable income. Do not assume an award or production grant is profit. Respect a desire for independence without promising it will be reached on an invented timeline.

## Compare relevant income streams
Consider existing artwork sales, editions, commissions, exhibition/participation fees, licensing, publications, teaching, research and compatible collaborations. Do not propose every stream by default. For each selected route, map buyer/commissioner, evidence of fit, actual deliverable, cost, rights, time to payment, uncertainty and next action. Teaching or brand work is not obligatory; the artist chooses acceptable tradeoffs.

## Offers and prices
Use documented current prices, edition availability and gallery agreements where they exist. Do not invent comparable sales from asking prices or auction headlines. Model a scenario range and separate retail price, gallery share, tax treatment, production cost and net amount. Public fee frameworks can inform a question, not substitute for the relevant country, contract or institution's terms.

## Controls
Maintain a consistent edition/consignment record. Review exclusivity, licences, usage duration, production obligations and payment milestones with Legal Office when consequential. Forecast cash by actual dates and confidence, not by multiplying imagined conversion rates. Grants and prospects stay in the pipeline until confirmed.

## Output
Present a small set of viable routes and a next 30/90-day action, with costs and unknowns. Use existing budget tools for arithmetic and qualified advisers for tax/legal conclusions. Report what must be declined or renegotiated as clearly as what could generate revenue. No payment, invoice send or contract acceptance occurs without actual authorization.
