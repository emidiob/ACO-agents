# Proportionate artwork records and preservation

Original ACO operational method · v0.6.0. Select for the relevant task; do not load every playbook.

## Scope
Manage the actual record and material lifecycle of a work, not a second speculative project database. Separate the work, its edition/instance, each media asset, exhibition event and ownership/loan relation. Do not make a new work ID for every image export.

## Minimal record
Retain an existing identifier, title, credited maker(s), date, medium, dimensions or duration with units, status, location, rights/credit information and canonical documentation links. Keep edition numbering and artist proofs separate from physical stock. When a fact is unknown, record unknown rather than inventing a completion date, acquisition or provenance.

Use controlled vocabularies such as Getty terms where they improve interoperability, while preserving the artist's own descriptions. An external identifier is optional; never automatically flatten a culturally specific term into a generic English category. Spectrum is a reference with its own licensing conditions, not a copied procedure set or a claim of ACO certification.

## Movement and condition
Check the actual item, current location, destination, packing, carrier, insurance responsibilities, condition evidence and return plan. A photograph alone may not reveal condition. Mark borrowed, reserved, consigned and sold distinctly and preserve the date/source of each transition. Financial valuations and legal status require qualified context; do not claim a formal appraisal.

## Time-based and software work
Distinguish artistic invariants from changeable implementation details. Record master files, checksums, codec/container, intended display and sound, reference installation, dependency/model versions and activation/licence requirements when needed. Ask which changes are acceptable to the artist. Preserve an original before a migration and compare the output; a checksum validates bytes, not aesthetic equivalence.

## Scale and storage
One compact record with links is sufficient for a small practice. Only propose Omeka, CollectionSpace or another collection system when scale, access and a maintenance owner justify it. An external catalogue remains authoritative; ACO stores its link and essential operational state, not a mirror. Do not strip or upload third-party rights notices. New tools are not automatically installed.

## Review
Can the next responsible person locate, identify and present the correct work? Are rights, ownership and editorial claims distinct? Can a proposed technical change be reversed? Save only durable updates to the existing canonical record; source assets are not cleanup candidates merely because they are large or old.
