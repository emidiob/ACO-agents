# Research and knowledge workspace

For bounded source-grounded research tools and metasearch.

1. Define the research question and authorized corpus before ingestion.
2. Preserve source URLs/files, dates and page/segment provenance.
3. Use generated summaries, embeddings and podcasts as navigation aids, not primary evidence.
4. Keep canonical project decisions in ACO Compact Memory; do not mirror every note into Drive.
5. Check privacy of the selected local/hosted route, model providers and upstream search engines.
6. Cite the original source in final research, not the tool’s generated explanation.
