# Finance operations with traceable evidence

## Scope
Operational budgets, project accounts and reconciliations; not investment advice, automatic bank actions, payroll decisions or a substitute for an accountant. Resolve period, currency, basis, entity and source versions first. No mixing company or client accounts.

## Procedure
1. Separate estimate, committed cost, actual expense, invoiced amount, cash received and forecast. Record data origin and cutoff time.
2. Reconcile independent records by stable identifiers and tolerances explicitly chosen for the task. Surface unmatched items, duplicates, timing differences, taxes/fees and currency issues; do not plug unexplained balances.
3. For a close packet, check completeness of receipts/invoices, accrual assumptions, opening/closing continuity and variances. Propose journal adjustments for human review; do not post them.
4. For production pricing, separate internal labour, external spend, contingency, agency fee and rights. Report margin versus markup correctly and distinguish cash timing from profitability.
5. Keep formulas/calculations reproducible and label assumptions. Use ACO's budget-check for supported arithmetic; it does not compute jurisdiction-specific taxes by itself.
6. Produce a concise exceptions register with owner and required evidence. Obtain specialist review for tax, employment, material reporting or legal uncertainty.

## Output
Reconciliation/variance table, source map, proposed adjustments and approval status. An exact calculation over incomplete inputs is not a verified financial position.
