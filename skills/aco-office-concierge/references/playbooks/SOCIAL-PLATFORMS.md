# Platform-specific editorial lenses

Original ACO operational method · v0.6.0. Select for the relevant task; do not load every playbook.

## Use this as an editorial lens, not a current API specification
Recheck provider/account capabilities, publishing formats, music licences, limits and monetization/disclosure rules at execution time. Nothing here guarantees reach. Do not hardcode algorithm explanations or numerical content limits from a repository.

| Platform | Content decision to test | Manual review | Evidence of usefulness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | What visual sequence or encounter is worth saving, sharing or viewing? Select still, sequence or moving image according to material. | Legibility, crops, caption context, artwork credits, licensed audio, alt text or an accessible equivalent. | Account-level outcomes tied to objective; separate paid reach. |
| TikTok | What action, process or point of view can be understood as moving image? | Opening clarity, sound-off comprehension, continuity, likeness/voice consent and context lost by shortening. | Watch behavior and relevant responses; no invented algorithm rule. |
| LinkedIn | What specific professional experience or evidence benefits this audience? | Verifiable claims, client confidentiality, meaningful point rather than manufactured autobiography. | Relevant conversations, qualified interest and actual referral evidence. |
| YouTube | What sustained question earns the running time? | Accurate title/thumbnail, chapter and source context, audio, captions, rights and whether a Short needs a different edit. | Retention and intended audience outcomes with denominators and dates. |
| X / Threads | What concise position or conversation is worth contributing to? | Context collapse, source accuracy, harassment risk and whether a thread improves clarity. Treat their capabilities separately. | Useful exchanges, referral behavior and response quality rather than outrage. |
| Pinterest | What visual research or practical discovery does the pin support? | Accurate destination, image permissions, comprehensible description and no misleading before/after. | Relevant outbound activity and downstream usefulness, not impressions alone. |

## Adapt a batch
Start from one approved source work or proposition. For each chosen platform specify the audience context, format, edit and a different reason to engage. Do not create six accounts or channels because a table lists six platforms. An artist may choose one low-frequency channel or none.

## Research and approval
Use current official documentation as the factual source for operations: Meta developer documentation, TikTok for Developers, YouTube documentation, LinkedIn developer documentation, X developer documentation and Pinterest developers. Check the exact product and permission in use. If the source cannot be retrieved, keep preparation possible but withhold unverified publication instructions.

Draft variants in chat or the existing calendar. Keep strategic rules in the same Brand/Social profile used by other agents. This workflow does not create separate platform agents, brand profiles or posting files.
