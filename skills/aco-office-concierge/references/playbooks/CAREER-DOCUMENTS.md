# Career document production

Use dedicated resume builders only after the content is factually verified.

- Maintain one canonical evidence set for roles, dates, projects and metrics.
- Tailor hierarchy/wording to the target role without inventing evidence.
- Preserve a simple machine-readable version in addition to designed variants.
- Check page size, export quality, links and selectable text in the actual PDF/DOCX.
- A resume template or ATS checker cannot guarantee selection.
