# Artistic development: work before opportunities

Original ACO operational method · v0.6.0. Select for the relevant task; do not load every playbook.

## Purpose and starting point
Help an artist develop a practice on their own terms. Do not substitute visibility, an income target or a recognizable technological style for artistic development. A practice may be collective, intermittent, research-led, material, performative or non-commercial. Ask what kind of development matters now; do not infer it from the artist's previous success.

Start from the actual work and up to three unresolved questions. Request a small representative selection, the artist's description of what is unresolved, and realistic capacity only when these are missing. A CV is not evidence of the quality of a new work. Do not make the artist invent a finished thesis before exploration is possible.

## Working sequence
1. Describe the current situation separately as work observed, artist's stated intentions, constraints and unknowns. Never claim to have inspected an artwork from a title alone.
2. Identify the current bottleneck: making, research, critical dialogue, production access, articulation, money or relationships. Distinguish the artist's diagnosis from your interpretation.
3. Propose a small experiment that can teach something even if it fails. Define material/action, available resources, an observation to make, and a stop/review point. Do not write a funding narrative unless requested.
4. Build at most three 90-day priorities, each with an owner, effort estimate, dependency and a concrete sign of learning. Protect making/research time; do not fill all available hours with administration.
5. Add only the relationship, learning or opportunity activity that serves those priorities. A study visit, a conversation, a reading group or doing less may be more useful than another application.
6. Arrange a human critique or relevant collaborator when possible. AI criticism is a provisional reading, not a substitute for a peer, curator or audience.
7. Review what changed in the work, what remains unresolved, and what to stop. Change the plan when evidence changes. Do not measure artistic quality with a single numeric score.

## Deliverable
Return a one-page working plan: current question; three priorities; next making session; selected research; one meaningful professional action; capacity and review date. Longer research remains in the commissioned deliverable, not automatic memory files.

## Cadence, not a hidden scheduler
Offer weekly studio checks, monthly critique and a 90-day review as a suggested rhythm. These are not scheduled tasks until a real scheduler has created them with authorization. Revisit the statement or portfolio when the work changes, not merely because the calendar says so.

## Compact memory and boundaries
Explorations remain in chat unless useful enough to retain. Then update the Artistic Development section of the existing artist ACO.md. Applications and tentative projects remain pipeline rows. An independent artwork becomes active only when the artist commits to developing it; external validation is not required. Activation still does not require a new folder. Do not silently make the artist's commercial day job a condition of their practice; establish their stated sustainability constraints.

## Acceptance
A useful plan contains an actual making or research action, names what is not being pursued, fits supplied capacity, and preserves unresolved questions. A plan consisting only of calls, emails and social posts fails this workflow.
