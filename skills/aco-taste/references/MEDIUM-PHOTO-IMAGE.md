# Photography, generative image and still visual quality contract

Direction: purpose/context, genre, camera viewpoint and optics where appropriate, moment/gesture, light source and falloff, texture/material, composition, crop, color relation, foreground/background, existing brand or series identity. Avoid contradictory lenses, composite lighting and incoherent camera moves. Inspect reference photos separately: relevance to look is not identity permission.

After producing an image, **actually inspect it**: subject and detail fidelity, hands/edges/text, proportions, lighting and shadow physics, weird textures, misleading branding and legibility. For image editing, compare the unchanged areas as well as the requested change. Across a series assess perspective, light direction, palette, subject/wardrobe and art direction continuity. Metadata and dimensions do not count as visual inspection.

If provider results are unavailable, return a production-ready prompt with an explicit unrendered status. Do not claim visual quality as tested. Use ACO Capture/Image as technical lead.
