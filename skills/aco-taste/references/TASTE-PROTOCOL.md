# ACO Taste — operating contract

## Universal trigger (not an extra command)
For a requested visual **deliverable** including film, video, photography, generative images, CGI, graphics, brand identity, posters, websites, apps, interfaces, social visuals, typography, publications, documents, PDF layout, presentations, exhibition/spatial visualization: load a bounded Taste capsule automatically. This also applies to ACO Genjutsu, Capture, Image, Video, Motion, Webdesign and related office specialists. A user's exact edit may omit concept exploration but never omit checking the changed part.

Do not start Taste just to answer an unrelated factual question containing a visual term; explanation-only work remains direct. An explicit user refusal of aesthetic redesign takes precedence. Taste must NEVER override locked identity, supplied photographs, established art direction or the requested limited modification.

## Seven gates
1. **Context** — Distinguish user-approved identity, supplied content/assets, observed references, inferred preferences, practical constraints, and unknowns. Identify one distinctive factual anchor for the design. Project scope applies; do not infer a universal personal taste.
2. **Direction** — For new/exploratory work, produce 2–3 structurally distinct concepts (not palette swaps), with materials, typography, layout, image/camera treatment, rhythms and disadvantages. For a locked route, skip exploration and preserve it.
3. **Critique before execution** — Compare every concept to the brief, desired audience, content, rights and existing design system. Reject a concept that can be assigned unchanged to a competitor, or whose hero hides the real product.
4. **Build** — Let the existing ACO specialist execute through tools actually available. Do not add a framework, buy assets, change remote accounts, generate media or install third-party dependencies without the applicable authorization.
5. **Inspect the real artifact** — For stills visually inspect images and series; for video inspect the timeline and contact sheet plus key transitions; for print/PDF inspect exported pages; for web/UI inspect rendered browser views and interaction states. Source code tests alone are insufficient.
6. **Fix** — Rank the three most significant observed problems by effect on the brief and make targeted corrections. Repeat inspection when substantial changes are made. A limit of three rounds is a useful cost budget, not a quality certification.
7. **Evidence-based delivery** — State what was produced, how the aesthetic goals relate to evidence, what was actually viewed, and what still needs review. Do not claim user approval, artistic merit or perfect quality.

## Review independence
A genuine second reviewer, blinded to which variation is newer, is preferable when supported. Without actual independent review, run a separate skeptical critique pass. Do not call it an independent expert or pretend a split prompt produces real human taste.

## Precedence
**Explicit user constraints → confirmed project `DESIGN.md`/identity → accessibility, safety, legal and rights → task-specific art direction → medium-specific ACO methods → candidate sources/reference galleries → optional third-party styles.** Sources can disagree and none of them supplies blanket aesthetic bans. An unusual brand choice may be correct. No blanket ban on black, Inter, gradients, centered layout, cards or animation.

## Modes
- **New/major:** direction → build → full reviewed delivery.
- **Existing small edit:** preserve identity → exact change → visually verify changed region and regressions.
- **Critique-only:** observe and report; no code/asset edits.
- **Limited evidence:** deliver a clearly marked draft/spec or uninspected artifact; do not label it visually verified.

## Risk/privacy
References can have unclear copyright, trademarks, people/likeness permissions and private-project data. Keep raw visuals in their authorized project. Never copy private client examples into public ACO skillpacks. No background monitors, persistent preferences or shared library writes without explicit scoped authorization.
