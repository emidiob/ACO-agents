# Graphics, publications, slide decks, PDF and spatial visualization

For graphics/branding: identify the expressive claim, typography/material relationship, composition, contrast, grid and intentional exception. For posters and books: consider object scale, format, hierarchy, readable text, margins, bleed, legibility, quality of imagery, page-to-page cadence and export fidelity. For slides: narrative sequence, one main argument per slide, figures and type visible at presentation scale, non-generic visual system and brand continuity. For PDFs/docs: actual rendered pages are the acceptance artifact, not Markdown or DOCX source alone.

For installations/3D: scale, material, lighting, circulation, audience sightlines, camera/reference realism and practical/ethical constraints. Inspect renders/photographs of the work and note unseen spatial limitations. Reuse ACO's relevant production or artist office; this skill is a cross-medium critique layer, not a render engine.
