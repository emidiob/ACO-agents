# publishing-office — role index

Lead: `publishing_media_orchestrator`. Read only selected role instructions; native names use `aco_`.

- [`acquisitions_editor`](agents/acquisitions-editor.md) — Assess and develop prospective titles and commissions.
- [`audience_development_editor`](agents/audience-development-editor.md) — Connect editorial work to readers without corrupting it.
- [`audiobook_producer`](agents/audiobook-producer.md) — Plan audiobook casting, recording, edit and distribution.
- [`author_relations_manager`](agents/author-relations-manager.md) — Coordinate authors and contributors professionally.
- [`book_distribution_manager`](agents/book-distribution-manager.md) — Plan book sales channels, orders and returns.
- [`branded_content_editor`](agents/branded-content-editor.md) — Produce clearly identified sponsored editorial formats.
- [`circulation_subscription_manager`](agents/circulation-subscription-manager.md) — Manage publication subscriptions and membership operations.
- [`copy_editor`](agents/copy-editor.md) — Perform consistent sentence-level and house-style editing.
- [`developmental_editor`](agents/developmental-editor.md) — Improve manuscript argument, structure and reader experience.
- [`ebook_accessibility_engineer`](agents/ebook-accessibility-engineer.md) — Build and validate accessible digital publications.
- [`editorial_designer`](agents/editorial-designer.md) — Design books, magazines and publication systems.
- [`journalist_reporter`](agents/journalist-reporter.md) — Research and draft sourced reporting.
- [`literary_translator`](agents/literary-translator.md) — Translate publication text with voice and terminology control.
- [`managing_editor`](agents/managing-editor.md) — Manage an editorial list, issue or publication pipeline.
- [`media_ad_operations_manager`](agents/media-ad-operations-manager.md) — Coordinate advertising inventory and delivery for a media publisher.
- [`newsroom_editor`](agents/newsroom-editor.md) — Manage reported coverage, verification and corrections.
- [`podcast_audio_producer`](agents/podcast-audio-producer.md) — Produce podcast episodes and audio series.
- [`prepress_specialist`](agents/prepress-specialist.md) — Prepare print-ready files and proof checks.
- [`print_production_manager`](agents/print-production-manager.md) — Coordinate publication manufacturing and physical distribution preparation.
- [`proofreader`](agents/proofreader.md) — Check final proofs against approved text and production requirements.
- [`publishing_media_orchestrator`](agents/publishing-media-orchestrator.md) — Route publishing & media tasks through a small accountable team with early clarification and verified outputs.
- [`publishing_metadata_specialist`](agents/publishing-metadata-specialist.md) — Prepare identifiers and discoverability metadata for publications.
- [`publishing_rights_permissions_manager`](agents/publishing-rights-permissions-manager.md) — Track text, image, translation and format permissions.
- [`typesetter`](agents/typesetter.md) — Apply approved publication design consistently.
- [`publishing_director`](../../aco-organization-office/references/agents/publishing-director.md) — Publishing director for the organization digital and print publications, editorial products, dossiers, books and distribution strategy.
- [`publication_manager`](../../aco-artist-office/references/agents/publication-manager.md) — Artist-publication production manager for monographs, catalogues, artist books and editorial projects: contributors, captions, permissions, proofing, print, ISBN, budgets, schedules and distribution.
- [`commissioning_editor`](../../aco-organization-office/references/agents/commissioning-editor.md) — Commissioning editor for external writers, artists, curators, designers, technologists and researchers contributing to the organization.
- [`editor`](../../aco-office-concierge/references/agents/editor.md) — Editor for artist statements, curatorial texts, applications, proposals, press releases, decks, publications, website copy and long-form cultural writing.
- [`art_writer`](../../aco-office-concierge/references/agents/art-writer.md) — Contemporary-art writer for original criticism, reviews, catalogue essays, artist profiles, exhibition texts, commissioned essays and intellectually serious cultural writing.
- [`research_editor_fact_checker`](../../aco-organization-office/references/agents/research-editor-fact-checker.md) — Research editor and fact checker for the organization publications, research outputs, decks and public claims.
- [`content_director`](../../aco-agency-office/references/agents/content-director.md) — Content director for brand editorial systems, content pillars, formats, franchises and channel ecosystems.
- [`community_editor`](../../aco-organization-office/references/agents/community-editor.md) — Community editor for the organization contributors, readers, participants, collaborators and recurring publics; focused on meaningful participation rather than vanity metrics.
- [`communications_pr_director`](../../aco-organization-office/references/agents/communications-pr-director.md) — Communications and PR director for the organization institutional narrative, press, launches, newsletters and public positioning.
- [`contracts_ip_manager`](../../aco-organization-office/references/agents/contracts-ip-manager.md) — Contracts and IP manager for the organization commissioning, client, contributor, partnership, licensing, image, data and AI terms; first-pass issue spotting, not legal representation.
- [`royalty_revenue_accountant`](../../aco-finance-office/references/agents/royalty-revenue-accountant.md) — Prepare reconciled author, artist and licensing royalty statements.
- [`art_director`](../../aco-office-concierge/references/agents/art-director.md) — Visual art director for photography, film, editorial, campaigns, exhibitions and image systems. Develops concrete visual direction rather than generic moodboards.
