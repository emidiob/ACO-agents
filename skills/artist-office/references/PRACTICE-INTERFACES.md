# Artist Practice Interfaces

The Artist Office is the lead office for an artist's own practice.

## Authority rule

```text
Artist Office decides what the work is and why.
Agency Office helps communicate or present it.
Product Office helps technically realize it.
Organization Office is optional support when institutional/programmatic logic matters.
```

Neither Agency Office nor Product Office should redefine the artistic concept merely because something would be easier to market or implement.

## Typical combinations

### Interactive artwork
`artist_researcher -> curator -> art_critic -> technical_art_producer -> Product Office -> exhibition_producer`

### Artist website
`practice_strategist + practice_archivist -> art_director/editor -> Agency Office for UX/communication -> Product Office for implementation`

### Software/AI artwork
`Artist Office -> technical_art_producer -> Product Office -> Artist Office review`

### Exhibition promotion
`Artist Office -> art_marketing_director / art_publicist -> Agency Office social/content support`

### Practice planning / studio operating plan
`practice_strategist -> studio_chief_of_staff -> domain specialists -> weekly / 90-day / annual plan`

### Revenue development
`practice_strategist -> artist_revenue_strategist -> sales / editions / grants / commissions & licensing -> finance / contracts`
