# Artist Office Briefing Guide

## Plan the practice

```text
Use the Artist Office with my Artist Context.
Plan my artistic practice for the next 12 months.
Assess where the practice stands, define artistic/career/institutional/production/revenue priorities, then create quarterly milestones and the next 90 days.
Protect time for making and research. Flag what should be stopped, postponed or declined.
```

## Build a 3-year strategy

```text
Use the Artist Office with my Artist Context.
Build a 3-year practice strategy, then work backwards into a 12-month plan and next 90 days.
Keep artistic development separate from career visibility and revenue, but make the tradeoffs explicit.
```

## Increase artist-side revenue

```text
Use the Artist Office with my Artist Context.
I want to increase sustainable revenue from my practice over the next 12 months without repositioning the work around commercial demand.
Map current and plausible income streams, identify what fits the practice, model net economics and opportunity cost, and give me a 90-day + 12-month action plan.
```

## Commission or license

```text
Use the Artist Office with my Artist Context.
Review this commission/licensing opportunity.
Separate artist fee, production costs and rights. Define usage, territory, duration, exclusivity, approvals, credit, deliverables, payment schedule, risks and negotiation points.
```
