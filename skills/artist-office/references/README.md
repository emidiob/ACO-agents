# Artist Office

Generic professional contemporary-artist office agents for artistic development, career, practice planning, revenue, production and studio operations. No artist identity, biography, taste or project history is embedded in the agents.

Use `contexts/private/artist-context.md` when continuity is needed. For critique, research and benchmarking, a blind first pass is often preferable.


See `PRACTICE-INTERFACES.md` for how the Artist Office recruits the Agency Office or Product Office without surrendering artistic authority.

## Practice planning + revenue

The Artist Office includes dedicated roles for keeping the practice artist-led while making it operational and financially sustainable:

- `practice_strategist` — long-horizon direction and portfolio-level tradeoffs.
- `studio_chief_of_staff` — translates strategy into 90-day, annual and weekly execution.
- `artist_revenue_strategist` — builds a diversified, practice-compatible revenue model.
- `commissions_licensing_manager` — structures commissions, licensing, reproduction rights and paid usage.

Typical invocation:

```text
Use the Artist Office with my Artist Context.
Plan my practice for the next 12 months, including artistic development, career, production, relationships and revenue. Protect making/research time and identify what should be stopped or deferred.
```
