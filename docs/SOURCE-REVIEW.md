# Source review and selection — 0.6.2

Review date: 2026-09-20. Sources are external and retain their own rights. This release contains original ACO task methods and links; it does not vendor upstream code, prompts, courses, standard text, fonts, model weights or datasets.

## What was reviewed

The 35 additions below were selected from publisher pages and repository overviews for fit with the requested studio work. Selected source methods informed the questions and checks in ACO. This was not a full source-code/security/license audit or an executed comparison of creative outputs. No star-count rankings or quality scores are claimed.

The prior 62 resource entries are preserved from the supplied 0.5.2 package, with an explicit inherited-provenance field. Their old review flags are not a claim that this consolidation re-audited each resource. Three unresolved identities remain blocked.

## Important corrections and boundaries

- Creative Capital’s professional-development page reports temporary unavailability of its written courses after the May 2026 Canvas change. Do not promise an accessible course merely because it is listed. Its public resource page is a reference, not course content included in ACO.
- Spectrum’s publisher distinguishes noncommercial/reference usage from commercial derivatives. This release gates the entry to consultation only and makes no conformance claim. Ask the publisher or qualified counsel about proposed reuse; an ordinary ACO execution approval cannot grant those rights.
- Collection/image metadata being available does not imply that every image/work/person is cleared for reuse. Check item-level rights.
- The current canonical Postiz project is linked, not a third-party fork. Its exact accounts, platforms and API scopes require current provider verification.
- n8n and other platforms may have product/version-specific source-available or commercial terms. No generic “all these repos are MIT” claim is made.
- Professional-development sources are situated. UK/US-origin advice, eligibility, artist fees and contracts must not be assumed universal.

## Current contemporary-art reading, not a single canon

Use relevant institutional/artist primary material alongside independently authored criticism and diverse geographic perspectives. Distinguish journal criticism from announcements, press releases, sponsor material and market reporting. Read actual available material; do not fabricate access to paywalled essays.

Examples consulted as reading sources: [e-flux Journal](https://www.e-flux.com/journal/), [ArtReview](https://artreview.com/), [Frieze](https://www.frieze.com/) and [Afterall](https://www.afterall.org/). These are optional sources, not automatically scraped archives or authority rankings. Add local-language and situated perspectives for the question at hand.

[W.A.G.E.](https://wageforwork.com/) provides a contextual artist-fee reference; it is not an international fee mandate. [WCAG guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/) can inform accessibility requirements; an automated scan is not proof of full conformance.

## Selection policy

Default to the host and existing tools. Scale-only entries such as CRM/work-management/archive platforms are separate adoption decisions. Reference-only entries may be consulted within applicable terms but are not authorized for execution, embedding or commercial adaptation through this registry. Others require exact source/version, license, environment, data and spend review before execution.
