## Reuse, not duplicate teams

Engineering was already present: Product Office retains software architecture, frontend/backend/fullstack/mobile, data/AI, QA, accessibility, security, DevOps, performance, release and documentation. Two new roles cover automation integration and MCP review.

Project managers/coordinators remain in Agency Office and are reused by Delivery. Existing executive/film/photo/post producers are reused by Production. Existing publisher/commissioning/editor roles are reused by Publishing. Finance reuses studio finance; Commercial reuses business development/account strategy. People Office supports the employer; Recruitment supports the job seeker.

For an artwork, the Artist Office protects artistic intent. For client work, the approved client/agency brief governs. Commercial metrics never silently rewrite an artwork or independent editorial conclusions.
