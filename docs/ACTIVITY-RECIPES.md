# ACO task recipes

Use ordinary language. Each recipe has an early brief check, a small possible team and explicit outputs. Not every helper should run. All external actions require real capabilities and authority.

## Plan an artistic practice

Workflow key: `practice-plan`. Lead method: `practice_strategist`.

**Resolve first:** Which artistic practice is this for? What should improve: making, opportunities, income, or a balance? How much time and budget are realistically available?

**Output:** Priority plan; Protected making time; Next actions.

**Gates:** Do not reshape the work around revenue without approval.

## Create a brand identity

Workflow key: `brand-identity`. Lead method: `brand_strategist`.

**Resolve first:** Which organization, client and brand is this for? What must this identity communicate and to whom? Which applications must be designed?

**Output:** Positioning; Distinct creative territories; Approved system and applications.

**Gates:** Approve strategic direction before production adaptations.

## Build a website

Workflow key: `website`. Lead method: `product_manager`.

**Resolve first:** Who owns this website and which project should we use? What should visitors be able to do? Is this a prototype or production website, and what content/features are essential?

**Output:** Requirements and flows; Working implementation; Test and deployment handoff.

**Gates:** No unapproved infrastructure spend or public deployment.

## Build or change software

Workflow key: `software`. Lead method: `tech_lead`.

**Resolve first:** Which codebase/product and approved workspace should be used? What behavior should be built or changed? Which stack, data, security or delivery constraints are binding?

**Output:** Implementation; Tests and evidence; Release notes.

**Gates:** Prototype is not production; no destructive migrations without authorization.

## Build an AI-enabled product feature

Workflow key: `ai-feature`. Lead method: `ai_product_engineer`.

**Resolve first:** What user task should AI accomplish? Which data may be used and sent to which services? What are the quality, cost and latency limits?

**Output:** Feature spec; Implementation and evaluation; Failure and escalation paths.

**Gates:** Consent/data boundary and evaluation before rollout.

## Cost and price a project

Workflow key: `estimate`. Lead method: `pricing_margin_analyst`.

**Resolve first:** Which entity/client/project is the quote for? What is included, in what quantities and usage scope? What costs/rates and currency are confirmed?

**Output:** Cost breakdown; Margin/markup scenarios; Quote draft.

**Gates:** Unknown taxes/rates remain unknown; owner approves offer.

## Plan studio/company cash flow

Workflow key: `cash-flow`. Lead method: `cashflow_treasury_analyst`.

**Resolve first:** Which entity and planning period? What cash is available versus restricted? Which receipts/payments and due dates are evidenced?

**Output:** Weekly cash plan; Downside scenario; Collection and timing actions.

**Gates:** No bank movement or financing commitment.

## Develop offers and a client pipeline

Workflow key: `sales-pipeline`. Lead method: `commercial_orchestrator`.

**Resolve first:** Which company/activity is selling? What are we offering and to which client segment? What capacity, geography, pricing floor or exclusions apply?

**Output:** Offer/lead plan; Qualified evidence; Outreach drafts and next actions.

**Gates:** No invented clients/cases; no unapproved outreach.

## Organize employer-side hiring

Workflow key: `hire`. Lead method: `people_orchestrator`.

**Resolve first:** Which employer and hiring decision owner? What tasks/skills and employment or freelance arrangement? What compensation band, location and start window?

**Output:** Job brief; Evidence-based sourcing; Human interview process.

**Gates:** No automated employment decisions; personnel confidentiality.

## Onboard a colleague or freelancer

Workflow key: `onboard`. Lead method: `onboarding_offboarding_coordinator`.

**Resolve first:** Which person/role and organization? What is the agreed start date? Which systems, equipment and access are approved?

**Output:** Onboarding checklist; Introductions/drafts; Access handoff.

**Gates:** No implied account creation/sharing authority.

## Recover a blocked or late project

Workflow key: `project-rescue`. Lead method: `project_manager`.

**Resolve first:** Which project and decision owner? What was approved and what is the actual status? What is blocked or at risk now?

**Output:** Recovery options; Revised forecast; Decisions and owners.

**Gates:** Do not rewrite approved baseline or promise dates without authority.

## Triage office work and correspondence

Workflow key: `office-triage`. Lead method: `executive_assistant`.

**Resolve first:** Which account and organization/personal scope? Which period and outcomes should this triage cover?

**Output:** Short priority brief; Ready-to-copy replies; Pending actions.

**Gates:** Read only the relevant threads; no auto-send.

## Prepare/send email

Workflow key: `email`. Lead method: `communications_operator`.

**Resolve first:** From which account and for which project? Who is the verified recipient? What is the exact message or call objective, and is this draft-only or authorized to send/call?

**Output:** Copy-ready draft or authorized action; Exact outcome/receipt.

**Gates:** Check actual email capability; No automatic channel switching; Reconcile uncertain previous outcomes before retry.

## Produce a film/photo/CG project

Workflow key: `production`. Lead method: `production_orchestrator`.

**Resolve first:** Which client/project and creative approver? What must be captured/generated and delivered? What budget, dates, rights and resources are available?

**Output:** Production plan; Crew/shot/cost plan; Delivery and handover.

**Gates:** Qualified physical safety review; Approve bookings and spend.

## Edit, finish and deliver video

Workflow key: `video-post`. Lead method: `postproduction_supervisor`.

**Resolve first:** Where are the actual source footage/cut and references? What is the intended story/look and final duration? Which aspect ratios, frame rate, color and audio delivery specifications?

**Output:** Versioned edit; Conform/grade/mix; QC and delivery.

**Gates:** Picture-lock and finishing approvals; A queued render is not completed.

## Retouch photography

Workflow key: `retouch`. Lead method: `photo_retouch_artist`.

**Resolve first:** Which original images and approved references? What corrections or changes are intended and forbidden? What output use, profiles, sizes and layer requirements?

**Output:** Retouch or precise retouch plan; Before-after review; Master/exports and notes.

**Gates:** Preserve originals and identity/product constraints.

## Build or repair a ComfyUI workflow

Workflow key: `comfyui`. Lead method: `comfyui_workflow_engineer`.

**Resolve first:** What output or workflow error are we solving? Which ComfyUI version, GPU/VRAM, installed nodes and models? Can we use the actual workflow/API export and relevant inputs?

**Output:** Verified graph or labeled blueprint; Dependency manifest; Preflight and test evidence.

**Gates:** Do not invent nodes/model files; No unapproved installs or paid queues.

## Publish a book or catalogue

Workflow key: `book`. Lead method: `managing_editor`.

**Resolve first:** What is the book for and who is the reader? What manuscript/images and rights are actually available? Which print/digital/audio formats, schedule and budget?

**Output:** Editorial/production plan; Edited/designed publication; Rights, metadata and delivery.

**Gates:** Author sign-off; Rights clearances; Print/release approval.

## Produce an editorial issue or newsroom package

Workflow key: `media-issue`. Lead method: `newsroom_editor`.

**Resolve first:** Which publication, audience and editorial question? Which verified sources, interviews or access are available? What format and publication deadline?

**Output:** Assignment and source plan; Verified drafts; Distribution and corrections plan.

**Gates:** Do not fabricate reporting; Separate advertising/sponsored content.

## Produce a podcast episode/series

Workflow key: `podcast`. Lead method: `podcast_audio_producer`.

**Resolve first:** What is the premise, audience and episode length? Who participates and what recordings/rights exist? Which languages/platforms and dates?

**Output:** Rundown; Audio or edit plan; Transcript and show notes.

**Gates:** Voice and guest consent; Actual mix/QC evidence.

## Manage publication subscriptions

Workflow key: `subscriptions`. Lead method: `circulation_subscription_manager`.

**Resolve first:** Which publication and authorized subscriber dataset? What should improve: onboarding, fulfillment, renewals or retention?

**Output:** Lifecycle plan; Cohort/report definitions; Customer drafts.

**Gates:** No unsolicited enrollment, silent billing or ignored opt-outs.

## Plan publisher advertising or sponsorship delivery

Workflow key: `media-advertising`. Lead method: `media_ad_operations_manager`.

**Resolve first:** Which publisher, sponsor/client and campaign? Which approved ad inventory or sponsored formats? What dates, labeling, budget and reporting terms?

**Output:** Insertion/delivery plan; Labeled assets; Delivery/billing report.

**Gates:** Editorial-commercial separation; No unapproved live trafficking.
