# A compact brand and social operating loop

Reuse an existing company's/client's Brand section. Keep legal entities, business activities, brands, offers and projects distinct without automatically giving each a folder.

## One authoritative profile

```text
Brand
- Audience, alternatives and context (with sources)
- Positioning options / approved direction
- Claims and proof: assumed / researched / validated
- Message hierarchy and voice examples
- Identity: type, colour, image, layout, motion and applications
- Candidate names and screening status (not false legal clearance)
- Social: objectives, chosen channels, formats, production capacity, consent and evidence
- Decisions, exceptions and next review
```

Link real design/source files and existing analytics tools; do not duplicate everything into Drive. A code project's `DESIGN.md` can be a real implementation contract, not a second universal memory file.

## Strategy to execution

Research before positioning. Compare distinct choices and tradeoffs. Get approval before claiming a direction is approved. Test the identity on actual content/applications and examine rendered output. Validate behavior, keyboard/accessibility and performance separately from aesthetic judgement. An approved hypothesis remains a hypothesis until tested.

For social, choose channels based on the practice/business and actual audience relationship, not an assumed algorithm or mandatory trend. Separate platform strategy from format production and publishing operations. Consult current official platform/provider documents for volatile media limits and API permissions. Use small experiments with baseline and data limitations rather than promises of virality.

## Publication

Final content/media/account/timing form a versioned packet. An approval for last week's copy or another account does not approve this packet. Verify rights, sources, privacy, accessibility and sponsorship disclosures. Use the actual connected account action only with applicable permission. On failure/timeout, reconcile before retrying. Queued, accepted and published are distinct states.

No connector? Provide copy-ready content, media specifications and suggested timing; put not-sent status outside the content. Do not create a fake receipt or post a screenshot of the intended interface as proof.
