# ACO Taste attribution and limits

ACO Taste contains original ACO instructions, data formats and command-line tools. Third-party implementations, fonts, photos and other assets are not bundled. The referenced projects, pinned revisions and recorded license information are listed in [`sources.json`](../skills/aco-taste/assets/sources.json). Check the rights of any external asset before adding it to a project.

## What the checks establish

The CLI checks routing, data formats, selected static properties and declared evidence. A passing check does not establish aesthetic quality, accessibility in assistive technology, animation performance or the accuracy of user-supplied content.

Inspect actual screenshots, images, rendered pages, video timecodes or exports for each deliverable. Record material issues and any checks that could not be run. Where the direction is consequential, seek human review of the rendered alternatives.
