- [`publication_manager`](../skills/aco-artist-office/references/agents/publication-manager.md) — Artist-publication production manager for monographs, catalogues, artist books and editorial projects: contributors, captions, permissions, proofing, print, ISBN, budgets, schedules and distribution.
