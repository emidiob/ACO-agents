# Publishing house and media company playbooks

## Books, catalogues and artist books
Resolve audience, editorial intention, manuscript/assets, rights, languages, print/digital/audio formats, print run assumptions, budget and deadline. Route commissioning/acquisition -> developmental edit -> source/fact check -> copy edit -> author approval -> design/typeset -> permissions -> proof -> prepress/EPUB/audio QC -> metadata -> printing/distribution -> release -> royalties/returns.

Reuse publishing_director, publication_manager and commissioning_editor. Add specialized execution roles only where useful. Manuscript approval, print approval and public release are separate decisions.

## Magazines, journals and editorial platforms
Use an issue/series/project record with commissioning calendar, article owners, verification status, image permissions, design/production, release schedule and corrections log. Independent reporting, criticism, opinion, sponsor-funded content and advertising must be labeled appropriately. Never invent interviews, sources or firsthand attendance. Sensitive allegations require evidence, fair attribution and appropriate human review.

## Newsletters and subscriptions
Prepare editorial role, audience promise, cadence, consented subscriber handling, distribution, retention and support. Keep lists private. Drafting a newsletter is not sending it; changing billing or subscription status is not authorized by creating a content plan. Track opt-outs and fulfillment rather than merely chasing open rates.

## Podcasts and audiobooks
Plan premise/audience, guest/voice rights, scripts or interview questions, recording, edit, proof-listen, sound, mix, transcription, metadata, release and distribution. Do not simulate a real interview as reporting. AI-generated/voice-cloned material requires suitable consent/rights and relevant disclosure; do not impersonate.

## Economics, rights and sales
Finance supports title P&L/cash scenarios, author advances/royalties, print costs, distributor discounts and returns. Commercial supports institutional sales, sponsorship and B2B offers. Publishing specialists manage identifiers/metadata, image/text/translation rights, authors, distribution, stock and subscription operations. No invented circulation, sales, book identifiers or rights clearance. A valid ISBN checksum is not an assigned ISBN.

## Typical records
PUBLICATION-CONTEXT; manuscript/style sheet; editorial calendar; permissions/credits; title/edition metadata; proof corrections; print/distributor quotes; stock/returns; author statements; subscriber policy; corrections ledger. Use blank templates to create private project records on demand. Do not put manuscripts, sources or subscriber data in the public ACO repository.
