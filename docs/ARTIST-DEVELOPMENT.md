# Artist development, without turning the practice into applications

Start from **work → inquiry → critical development → relationships/support**, not “deadline soon → invent a project.” The artist remains the decision-maker. ACO can help articulate options and practical needs; it must not manufacture an artistic identity from old context.

## The smallest useful team

`practice_strategist` leads the review. `art_critic` inspects actual work and proposes counter-readings. `curator` examines relationships between work, context, space and audience. `artist_agent` studies institutional affinity and meaningful contact. A researcher, producer, writer or finance role joins only for a concrete need.

Do not simulate agreement from famous curators or call a group of role prompts a real peer review. Bring substantive questions to actual peers and record only feedback the artist selects as useful.

## A practical first cycle

1. Inspect a small set of actual works/excerpts and what the artist is trying to test. Distinguish seeing a work from reading its description.
2. Separate unresolved artistic questions, technical bottlenecks, missing critical dialogue, capacity constraints and professional/financial needs.
3. Choose at most three next priorities. Define one immediate experiment, protected making/research time and a review moment.
4. Only then choose appropriate support: studio visit, collaborator, reading, archive/facility access, residency, exhibition or commission. Say what to decline and why.
5. Retain decisions and the next question in the existing practice record. Proposed work remains a pipeline entry; a residency submission is not an active artwork.

## Compact section, not extra documents

Add only useful fields to the existing Artist `ACO.md`:

```text
Artistic Development
- Current question (provisional, not permanent identity)
- Actual work and what was learned
- Unresolved critical questions / useful counter-readings
- Selected research horizon and references
- Up to three priorities and protected making time
- Relationship/support needs with a meaningful next action
- Opportunity pipeline (considering/submitted/declined/etc.)
- Next review date (not an installed schedule)
```

A 90-day review is a planning choice, not a requirement. Do not rewrite a statement every month if the practice has not changed. Do not turn every insight into a task or measure development only by visibility.
